Tall Tree is a culture publication about the Peninsula and the people who make it.

Named after El Palo Alto, the redwood that gave the city its name, Tall Tree goes backstage with the people behind the places we pass every day: the bookstore, the diner, the studio, the startup. Each story is told first on film, then deepened through photography and writing.

As emerging tech reshapes Silicon Valley, Tall Tree asks what stays human here, and who is keeping it that way.

I founded Tall Tree and lead its brand, editorial direction and production.

Launching now. Stay tuned.
