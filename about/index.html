About

Hello, I’m Sarah.

I’m a designer and storyteller who makes complicated things clear.

I studied Integrated Design and Media at NYU Tandon, and I work where design, technology, and people meet. For the past year I’ve run communications and growth for a contemporary art gallery in Palo Alto, producing video, campaigns, and public programs, and building AI-assisted workflows that give the team its time back.

Outside of work I make documentary films about artists and curate community events. I’m looking for a team building something worth explaining, in AI, technology, or culture.
