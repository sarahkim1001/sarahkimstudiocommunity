From Intern to Lead Social Content Producer

Entering the gallery as an intern, I saw untapped narrative potential in our artist roster. Observing the curiosity of our community, I pitched and scaled a new workflow—moving from static posts to leading end-to-end production for artist studio visits and in-depth interviews.

By researching each artist’s history, studying their technical practice, and listening closely during studio dialogues, I learned to distill complex creative philosophies into digestible, visually arresting stories. I owned the full process: pre-production research, on-site filming, and editing cinematic short-form Reels that capture spatial transitions, tactile texture, and the ideas behind the work.
